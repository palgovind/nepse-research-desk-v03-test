Accounting note

Buy charges and entered buy-side tax are added to acquisition cost. Sell charges and entered sell-side tax reduce net sale proceeds. Dividends, bonus/rights shares, splits, mergers and statutory tax calculations are not yet automated. Verify tax treatment and broker statements independently.

Data source policy

Official NEPSE API access is governed by NEPSE's data-use procedure and may require a fee and written approval. A public repository's software licence does not automatically license the underlying exchange data.

NEPSE official website · YONEPSE public dataset documentation (unofficial)

This build intentionally does not scrape NEPSE, bypass access controls, or call an unofficial feed. A connector can be enabled after its data rights, terms, provenance and permitted personal-use scope are confirmed.

Local storage & privacy

Imported data, portfolio transactions and settings stay in this browser on this device. They are not synchronised or backed up automatically. Export a backup before clearing browser data or changing devices.
